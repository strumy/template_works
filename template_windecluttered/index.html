Why WinDeCluttered?

WinDecluttered is a tool to optimize windows settings and uninstall unnecessary applications. Windows comes cluttered with many pre-installed unnecessary applications, marketing and advertising settings turned on. WinDecluttered helps to remove such applications, reorganizes your PC drivers and turn off marketing settings.

For more information please visit, Project Windecluttered developed by ArticulatedLogic
